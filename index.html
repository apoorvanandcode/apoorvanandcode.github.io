Blue

Blue is a personal expense tracker for Android that reads your bank SMS. It turns debit, credit and UPI messages into entries you confirm with a swipe, and shows where your money goes.

  • Scans your SMS by itself every day, at a time you choose.
  • You review each entry: category, who it was for, and the amount.
  • Insights by category, account, person and merchant.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Optional backup to your own Google Drive.

Blue is open source: github.com/apoorvanandcode/blue. It has no account, no ads and no server. Optionally, you can add your own Google Gemini API key so Blue can read SMS its built-in rules don't recognise.

Why Blue asks for Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional. Blue uses it for two features only: saving a backup of your entries to your own Google Drive, and exporting your entries to a Google Sheet. Blue can only access files it creates; it cannot see anything else in your Drive.
